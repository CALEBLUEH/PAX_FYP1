Jump onto the near step, then the upper ridge. Read the Keeper's bowl and press **F** there. All three authored lamp visuals turn green. The original payoff reads: **“Three lamps burn steady. A route kept for strangers is a home offered forward.”** The sandbox remains open. F6 resets the field notes/activation only, keeping construction, finite stocks and save files; lamps return dormant until the mission completes again.
