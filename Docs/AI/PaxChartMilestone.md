# Lowtide: the Lamplighter's Chart

18 October 2026 trip rotation 5, isolated `CodexWorkspace` from `7e4dce5`. One bounded content milestone: restore a mixed-material route, traverse it, and recover an original inland chart. No new economy, inventory, crafting or save framework.

## Player loop

Open `Assets/PAX/Scenes/PaxLamplighterChart.unity`. At the camp, mine five Timber blocks from the south row and three Stone blocks from the north row. Read the departure log's amber mark. Press **2 Timber**, approach the near bank, and extend the five amber crossing cells outward. For side-face building, use **Ctrl precise**, orbit Q/E to view the last tile's forward face, then RMB that face. Walk onto each new tile before extending the next. **R** returns to camp if you fall into the low channel; it does not reset stocks or respawn supplies.

Across the channel, read the old mooring mark. Press **Home** to reset the view, then **3 Stone** and restore the three cyan stair cells (far base, upper step, then near step keeps a clear placement view from the west). Jump onto the first step, then the lookout deck. Read the chart and press **F** on its mark. The destination has a saved canopy, chart board and three inland route marks. The discovery reveals **Ember Reach**, the ridge of three lamps, as original future context; no additional stage is created in this milestone.

Repairs require **exact** IDs/materials: five Timber crossing cells and three Stone stair cells. A physically solid wrong-material tile remains buildable/walkable but does not count toward the objective. Mine it to recover its actual material unit and replace it correctly. The right HUD reports both groups and all three discoveries. The existing finite resource rules, five-choice palette, stockpile HUD, green/red targeting, survey session behavior and post-completion sandbox stay in use.
