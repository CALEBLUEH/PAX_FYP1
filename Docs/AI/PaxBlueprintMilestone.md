## Transaction and compatibility

Single and multi-cell placement use one extracted geometry check for material, bounds, reach, occupancy, player clearance and physical line of sight. Every blueprint cell is checked before world or stock mutation. Existing support policy is then resolved outward from world-supported cells; planned neighbors may support each other only if their connected group reaches existing world support. A floating group cannot self-support. Aggregate per-material availability is validated before any prefab staging.

The world rechecks all cells/materials, stages inactive prefab instances and discards them on setup failure. Once all are ready it publishes the bounded batch in one synchronous call with one world revision, then charges the prevalidated exact aggregate cost. There is no frame/coroutine/save boundary inside the transaction. Protected/occupied cells cannot be overwritten. Pointer requests also require a current reachable target and the neutral input gate; no active blueprint means the earlier single-cell path. Earlier scenes have no blueprint owner or additional serialized dependency.
